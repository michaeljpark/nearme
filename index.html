Strategic Value

Proximity has no digital channel

E-commerce wins on digital targeting. Physical retail wins on proximity. But proximity has no digital channel. Merchants can promote products online, yet can't reach a customer standing 200 meters away with a real-time offer. Shoppers can browse flyers and apps, yet can't see what's actually available around them in the moment.

Near Me is a real-time, geolocation-based deals platform that activates proximity as a digital channel — built to accelerate clearance cycles, optimize inventory turnover, and establish a unified commercial data layer between merchants and shoppers at the moment of intent.

Problem Definition

The channel best suited to physical retail's biggest strength remains underserved

As e-commerce expands, physical retail is losing ground in a way that's often overlooked. The gap isn't price or selection — it's the integration between digital channels and physical presence. Online platforms target users based on real-time behavior and intent, while a brick-and-mortar store's strongest natural advantage — being close to the customer right now — has no equivalent digital infrastructure to activate it.

Merchants can promote products online, but they can't reach a nearby customer with a personalized, time-sensitive offer at the moment of intent. Shoppers can't see what's actually available within walking distance right now. Existing tools like flyer apps surface this week's deals at a city level — not what's happening on the street they're standing on. The result is a structural gap where proximity, physical retail's greatest advantage, has no digital infrastructure to activate it.

Design Strategic Value

A real-time commercial data layer is becoming necessary

Three converging signals suggest the infrastructure Near Me addresses is not optional — it is overdue.

Signal 01
Real-Time Clearance Is Scaling in Narrow Verticals
Flashfood and Too Good To Go prove that location-aware, time-sensitive offers move inventory fast — but each solves only one slice of the problem: near-expiry food. The model works. The platform layer doesn't exist yet.
Signal 02
Hyperlocal Commerce Is Re-Emerging
15-minute city planning, post-pandemic local consumption, and walking-distance discovery are all rising. Digital tools to support these behaviors lag e-commerce by a decade.
Signal 03
Commercial Data Remains Fragmented
Location, inventory, price, and promotion data live in siloed systems. No unifying layer combines them into a real-time, location-aware channel — the infrastructure gap Near Me is designed to close.

Together, these signals point to one conclusion: the infrastructure that unifies real-time, location-aware commerce is about to become essential, and it does not yet exist.

Design Strategies

Horizon Roadmap

H-1
Foundation
Anchor partner secured, white-label MVP embedded
0 – 12 months
Key Moves
  • Land LOI / Pilot with anchor retailer — Loblaws, Empire, Metro, or Canadian Tire
  • Build white-label SDK or embedded module
  • Geo core to ±50m accuracy
  • Partner store-network data pipeline
KPIs
  • In-app deal engagement rate
  • Store-visit conversion attributable to Near Me
  • Pilot NPS from partner side
Watch

Exclusivity clause scope — keep narrow (e.g., "grocery vertical, 18 months") so H-3 expansion stays open. Retain Near Me brand and IP ownership.

H-2
Scale Core Loop
Embedded launch live, geo optimized, revenue flowing from partner
12 – 24 months
Key Moves
  • Launch inside partner loyalty app — PC Optimum, Scene+, moi, or Triangle
  • Cover all banner stores across partner network
  • Geo upgrade: indoor positioning, dwell-time, route prediction
  • Revenue: SaaS license fee + attributed-sales rev share
KPIs
  • Feature usage within partner MAU
  • Attributed sales lift vs. control group
  • Indoor geo accuracy at scale
Watch

Must prove measurable lift to unlock H-3 expansion budget — instrument attribution from day one.

H-3
Multi-Tenant Platform
Second partner onboarded, platform identity established
24M+
Key Moves
  • Add category-non-compete partner — Canadian Tire if H-1 was grocer, or vice versa
  • Re-architect for multi-tenancy: per-tenant data isolation, shared geo intelligence layer
  • Brand shift from "feature of X" to "platform powering Canadian retail"
KPIs
  • Time-to-onboard 2nd tenant
  • Cross-tenant geo model performance
  • % revenue from non-anchor tenant
Watch

Brand positioning work must begin at H-2. The identity shift is the hardest transition — "feature" to "infrastructure."
