Mutually Exclusive · Collectively Exhaustive

How retailers reach customers today

Four channel families. Thirteen sub-channels. One structurally unoccupied node.
Key observation

Of 13 channel sub-nodes mapped across four mutually exclusive families, twelve are operated by mature incumbents. Real-time proximity — hyperlocal, intent-aware, in-the-moment offer delivery — remains structurally unoccupied. Flyer apps stop at the city level; loyalty platforms target behavior but not location-in-time. The remaining whitespace is the channel best suited to physical retail's biggest natural advantage.
